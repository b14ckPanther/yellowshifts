## 3. Adversarial Attack Surface Analysis

### 3.1 Timing & Replay Attacks
- **Mitigation**: QR presence proof tokens incorporate a dynamic hash generated by the physical kiosk, valid for at most 30 seconds. Once scanned and verified by the server, the challenge record is consumed atomically, preventing replay attacks.

### 3.2 Network Dropped Connection & Double-Tap Submission
- **Mitigation**: The employee attendance screen employs in-flight mutex locking (`_isProcessingAttendance`) and an automated reconciliation strategy: if a network drop or timeout occurs, the client queries `getMyOpenAttendance` before reporting an error, resolving success if the mutation actually committed.

### 3.3 Offline Kiosk Scanning Prevention
- **Mitigation**: When a kiosk tablet loses connection, the QR display is immediately obscured with an offline alert, preventing employees from scanning stale codes.
